📊 Dashboard Structure

The Power BI workbook is organized into 3 purpose-built operational reporting pages:

🏠
1. Home Page
35 Visual Components

High-level executive workforce overview and demographic breakdown.

  • Total Employee headcount & gender split
  • Department distribution & headcount share
  • Promotion readiness breakdown
  • Job satisfaction level distribution
⚡
2. Action Page
19 Visual Components

Operational decision rosters for HR management and department heads.

  • Employees due for promotion action list
  • Retrenchment risk monitoring roster
  • Departmental & Gender slice filters
  • Service tenure metrics
📋
3. Details Page
28 Visual Components

Granular talent analysis and operational performance metrics.

  • High-rated vs. low-rated employee percentages
  • OverTime workload distributions
  • Role-by-role compensation & level matrix
  • Individual employee service breakdowns

💡 How to Open & Refresh in Power BI Desktop

  1. Download the repository ZIP or the .pbix file above.
  2. Open Power Bi Practice Dashboard.pbix in Power BI Desktop.
  3. To connect to local CSV files on your machine: Click Home → Transform Data → Data Source Settings → Change Source and point to your local CSV directory.
